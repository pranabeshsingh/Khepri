# Welcome to Khepri Discussions!

We use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/pranabeshsingh/Khepri/discussions) as the primary community space for asking questions, sharing hardware setups, proposing new ideas, and connecting with other developers running local AI on the edge.

---

## Discussion Categories

To keep conversations organized, please choose the most relevant category when starting a new discussion:

* 📢 **Announcements:** Official news, new version releases, and major architectural milestones published by project maintainers.
* 💡 **Ideas & RFCs:** Share feature ideas, suggest new agent tools, or propose performance optimizations. This is the best place to get community feedback before opening a pull request.
* ❓ **Q&A:** Need help installing Khepri on your Raspberry Pi? Encountering configuration issues with your Telegram bot or GGUF quantization? Ask here!
  * *Tip:* When someone provides a solution that solves your question, please click **"Mark as answer"** so others can find it quickly.
* 🙌 **Show and Tell:** Share your real-world setups! Show off your Raspberry Pi 4/5 hardware enclosures, custom agent system prompts, Home Assistant integrations, or benchmark results.
* 💬 **General:** Open-ended conversations about small language models (SLMs), low-power computing, and the future of private edge intelligence.

---

## Issues vs. Discussions

To keep our issue tracker actionable for development, please refer to this guide:

| Use **GitHub Discussions** for: | Use **GitHub Issues** for: |
| ------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| "How do I configure Khepri with my CalDAV server?" | "CalDAV parser crashes with `ValueError: invalid rrule` on line 97" |
| "What GGUF model gives the best tokens/sec on Pi 4?" | "Memory leak in `pithon serve` causes OOM after 50 requests" |
| "Idea: Can we add a tool for Home Assistant integration?" | "Approved RFC implementation task" |
| "Sharing my 3D-printed Raspberry Pi 4 cooling enclosure" | "Broken Dockerfile build on linux/arm64" |

---

## Community Etiquette

1. **Search Before Asking:** Another community member may have already asked and answered your question.
2. **Be Specific:** When asking for troubleshooting assistance, share your hardware model, OS version, Python version, model quantization, and relevant logs.
3. **Be Respectful:** All discussions are governed by our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Treat everyone with kindness, empathy, and constructive encouragement.

We are excited to build the edge AI ecosystem together!
